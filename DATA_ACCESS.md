# Dataset access statement

The self-constructed complex sea-state dataset used to train and evaluate the
ADPD-DETR model is not included in this repository. The trained checkpoint is
also omitted from this source-only archive.

The dataset and corresponding trained checkpoint may be made available by the
corresponding author upon reasonable request. Requests should identify the
requesting researcher and institution, describe the intended non-commercial
research use, and confirm compliance with any applicable privacy, safety,
attribution, and redistribution restrictions.

Availability remains subject to institutional approval and any third-party
rights associated with the source imagery. The definitive contact details are
those listed for the corresponding author in the associated article.

The dataset configuration template and expected directory structure are
provided in `configs/datasets/complex_sea.yaml` and `README.md`. This source
archive does not contain a public download URL, access token, hidden credential,
or substitute dataset.
